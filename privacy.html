BIMMER COCKPIT

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Bimmer Cockpit (“the app”) is the companion app for the Bimmer Cockpit module, a self-built device fitted behind the dashboard of a car with a Siemens MS43 engine computer. The app talks to that module over a USB cable or over the module's own local Wi-Fi network.

Data the developer collects

None. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising or crash reporting, and it never sends data to the developer.

Data the app handles on your device

  • Module status and settings: temperatures, fan speed, AC state, engine data, warning lamps and thermostat settings. They are read from and written to the module, shown on screen, and the settings are stored on the module.
  • Engine logs: when you record (by hand, or automatically while the engine runs), the engine data and AC state are saved as CSV files in the app's own storage on the head unit. They stay there until you delete them in the app or uninstall it.
  • ECU data: when you tap Read ECU data, the app reads the engine computer's part number, stored fault codes and learned fuel and octane adaptations over the K-line and saves them, dated, as a JSON file next to the engine logs. It stays there until you uninstall the app.
  • Engine diagnosis: analysing a log runs entirely on the device. Nothing is uploaded for it. The analysis keeps a small summary of each analysed drive (a few averages such as idle speed and fuel trim) in the app's own storage, so later drives can be compared with earlier ones. It stays there until you uninstall the app.

Google Drive (optional)

If you tap Connect Drive, the app asks you to sign in with Google and grant access to Google Drive with the drive.file permission, which only covers files the app itself creates. The app then uploads your engine logs and ECU data files to an “Bimmer Cockpit logs” folder in your own Google Drive, automatically if Auto-upload is on. The files go directly from your device to your Google account; the developer never receives them or your Google account details. Google's handling of that data is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

You can turn off Auto-upload or tap Disconnect Drive in the app at any time, and remove the app's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files already in your Drive stay there until you delete them.

Sharing

When you use Share on a log or a diagnosis report, the app hands it to the app you choose through Android's share sheet. What happens next is up to that app.

Permissions

  • USB: to talk to the module over its cable.
  • Location (Android 10–12) and Nearby Wi-Fi devices (Android 13+): Android requires these for an app to find and join a Wi-Fi network. The app uses them only to connect to the module's hidden network and never reads or stores your location.
  • Internet / network state / Wi-Fi state: to talk to the module over its local network, and to upload logs to Google Drive when you have connected it.

Children

The app is not directed at children.
